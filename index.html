Common questions

Clear answers before you send plans.

What should I include with my request?

Send the available plans, specifications, addenda, target bid date, and any scope or pricing instructions. A planroom or blueprint link is optional but helps us review the project context.

What does the estimate package include?

Depending on the requested scope, deliverables can include quantity takeoffs, organized material and labor pricing, exclusions, alternates, and bid support for clarifications or revisions.

How is my project information handled?

Project details are treated as confidential and are used to review and respond to your request. Use a plan link your team can access without requesting permission.

Which project types do you support?

The intake supports commercial, industrial, multi-family, tenant improvement, and ground-up project types. Share the project context and we can confirm the appropriate review scope.
